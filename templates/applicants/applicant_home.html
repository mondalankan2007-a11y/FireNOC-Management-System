Applicant Portal

Welcome to the Fire Department Applicant Portal. Here, building owners and authorized representatives can apply for a Fire No Objection Certificate (NOC) and track the real-time status of their applications throughout the inspection and approval lifecycle.

Note: The "New Application" and "My Applications" actions are UI placeholders. Complete application filing, document uploads, and tracking features will be implemented in upcoming commits.
1. Apply for Fire NOC

Fill out building specifications, occupant capacity, and submit required architectural and fire safety diagrams.

2. Track Progress

Monitor desk verification, inspection scheduling, officer observations, and safety clearance milestones.

3. Receive Certificate

Download digitally authorized Fire NOC certificates with unique verification identifiers upon successful inspection.
