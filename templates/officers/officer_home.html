Fire Officer Module

Welcome to the Fire Officer & Inspection Portal. This module is used for application review, document verification, inspection, deficiency management and approval workflow.

Status: Foundation Initialized Coming Soon
Commit 1: Officer Module Architecture

Notice: The core Django structure for the Fire Officer module has been successfully initialized. Operational features including officer authentication, inspection scheduling, checklist audits, deficiency recording, and approval workflows will be implemented in subsequent commits.

1. Review & Verification

Scrutinize submitted building dimensions, architectural drawings, fire hydrant schemes, and applicant documents prior to site visit.

2. Inspection & Deficiencies

Perform on-site compliance audits of extinguishers, alarm systems, smoke detectors, and issue deficiency notifications where required.

3. Approval Workflow

Compile inspection observations, submit recommendations, and advance compliant applications for digital Fire NOC issuance.
